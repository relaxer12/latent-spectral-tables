Changes from spektrafilm, by relaxer12 (Latent)
==============================================

2026-11-02

Baked with spektrafilm 0.3.4 (commit 3bb2c2d) from its film profiles:

  kodak_portra_160, kodak_portra_400, kodak_portra_800, kodak_ektar_100,
  kodak_gold_200, kodak_ultramax_400       printed on kodak_portra_endura
  fujifilm_pro_400h                        printed on fujifilm_crystal_archive_typeii
  kodak_ektachrome_100, fujifilm_velvia_100  scanned as slides
  kodak_vision3_500t, kodak_vision3_250d   printed on kodak_2383

Run per pixel only (lut_mode: no grain, halation, glare or spatial effects), with the
profiles unchanged. What differs from spektrafilm's own rendering:

- Input. The tables take an iPhone's sRGB picture, already tone-mapped for a screen, so
  each 8-bit code is given the linear exposure at which the printed film comes out as
  bright as Latent's own ("classic") version of the stock makes that code. Brightness and
  contrast therefore follow Latent's curves; the colour is the film's.

- Print balance. The enlarger's yellow and magenta filtration is shifted from the
  database's neutral so a middle grey prints in the classic stock's colour (for example
  yellow -10.4 CC for Gold 200, about -2 CC for Portra). Slides get a scanner gain on each
  colour instead.

- Levels. A neutral scanner levels step sets the print's black and white to the classic
  stock's. For each exposure error, each colour's black and white first go where they sit
  when the film is exposed right; slides keep their error.

- Exposure. Seven tables a film, for -4 to +2 stops of exposure error, with the print
  exposure compensated as a lab would (negatives).

- Film data (-spectral.json). The grey-axis exposure for each code; each layer's silver
  density along a span of exposure, read from the profile's density curves through the
  film's own log exposure; spektrafilm's DIR coupler matrix and diffusion settings and
  in-emulsion scatter settings, as its parameters set them for each film.

- Grain (-spectral-grain.bin). Each dye layer's density noise from the film's datasheet
  RMS granularity (the values in spektrafilm's experimental branch, presets/grain.toml),
  scaled from the 48 µm aperture to the pixel (Selwyn) and with density by D(Dmax - uD);
  then taken to each output colour by the print's response to that layer's density,
  measured by nudging the density through spektrafilm's pipeline taps.
